FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Marwa Digital.

How long does it take to see SEO results? +
SEO is a long-term investment. Most clients begin seeing meaningful improvements in rankings and traffic within 3–4 months. Significant, compounding results typically emerge between months 6–12. We set realistic expectations upfront and provide transparent monthly reporting so you can track progress every step of the way.
Do you work with businesses of all sizes? +
Absolutely. We work with startups, small businesses, and large enterprises across multiple industries. Our Starter plan is perfect for growing businesses, while our Enterprise plan is tailored for established brands seeking aggressive scale. Every strategy is custom-built for your specific stage and goals.
What makes Marwa Digital different from other agencies? +
We're obsessed with measurable outcomes, not vanity metrics. Every strategy we implement is tied to your business objectives — whether that's revenue, leads, or brand awareness. We combine deep industry expertise with cutting-edge tools, radical transparency, and a genuine passion for helping our clients succeed.
Is there a minimum contract length? +
We recommend a minimum 3-month commitment for digital marketing services, as this is the time required to implement strategy, gather data, and begin optimizing for results. However, we don't believe in locking clients in against their will — we earn your continued business through exceptional results.
How do you measure and report on success? +
You'll receive comprehensive monthly reports covering all agreed KPIs — organic traffic, keyword rankings, lead volume, conversion rates, ROAS, and more. We also set up a live dashboard you can access anytime. For Growth and Enterprise clients, we hold regular strategy calls to discuss performance and next steps.
Can I start with a free audit? +
Yes! We offer a comprehensive free digital marketing audit for all new inquiries. This includes an SEO health check, website performance analysis, competitor research, and a customized growth roadmap — all at no cost and with zero obligation. Simply fill out the contact form below to get started.
